Mahon

Mahon is a capity of Menorca

Informations Mahon officially Maó, locally and also written as Mahon or Port Mahon in English, is the capital and second largest city of Menorca. The city is located on the eastern coast of the island.

Most of the restaurants there are open from 12:30 to 3:30. Then there is a siesta for two hours and they opening again at 7:30 up to 11:00.

In Spain there is a National Holiday called Fiesta and it is on 12.10.
